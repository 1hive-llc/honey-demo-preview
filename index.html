02 / FOLLOW AN APPEAL

How an initial ruling can change

This separate worked example summarizes a synthetic test. Expand each stage to follow the funds.

First round: three seats favor the applicant

One test juror held all three seats. That tally was provisional, not three independent opinions.

Funding: one side is not enough

A backer contributes 0.63 test HNY for the challenger. No second round opens until the other side also funds its choice with 0.42. Of the 1.05 total, 0.21 covers round fees and 0.84 remains.

New round: the ruling reverses

One test juror holding seven seats favors the challenger. After the appeal period, the test agreement cancels the decision.

Settlement: who receives the remaining 0.84?

The successful appeal backer receives 0.84 test HNY, including their original 0.63. The other backer receives zero. This payment is not a juror reward.
